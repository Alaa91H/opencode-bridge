# v3.36.0 — `/metrics` live operational metrics (2026-10-17)

## Changes

- `TaskQueueStore.queue_counts()` (index-covered `GROUP BY status`) and
  `audit_log.read_recent()` (bounded tail, skips partial lines).
- `BridgeMetricsService` snapshots queue depth, running/worker ceiling,
  outcomes and deferral reasons in a window, plus host memory/CPU/disk —
  on demand, with no background sampler and no new listening port.
- `/metrics [hours]` in the command menu, `/help`, the control panel's
  advanced/server section, and the legacy dispatch allowlist.
- `tests/test_metrics_command.py`; control-UI coverage contract extended.

## Bounds

Window 1–720 hours (default 24). Outcome figures read at most the last
256 KiB of the audit log; queue gauges come from SQLite and are exact.

## Upgrade

No deps, no migrations. Read-only command. Source release only.
